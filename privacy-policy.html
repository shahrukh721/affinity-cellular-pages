Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Affinity Cellular® (“Affinity Cellular,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information about visitors to this website and individuals who contact us about our wireless service plans and promotional offers. By using this website, calling us, or submitting your information through any form on this site, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, telephone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Inquiry details, such as your current wireless carrier, approximate monthly bill, number of lines, and the plans or offers you are interested in.
  • Communications records, including records of calls, text messages, and emails between you and our team, which may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance.
  • Technical information collected automatically, such as IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and referring website.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and check your eligibility for offers, including the 3 Months Free promotion;
  • Provide, activate, bill, and support wireless service;
  • Send you marketing and informational communications where you have consented to receive them;
  • Improve this website and our offers;
  • Detect and prevent fraud; and
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who work on our behalf, such as order fulfillment, customer support, analytics, and website hosting providers, who are bound to use your information only to provide services to us;
  • Network partners as necessary to activate and provide wireless service;
  • Legal authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and the safety of others; and
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with, or sold to, any third parties.

4. SMS, Calls, and Email Communications

By providing your telephone number through this website or by calling us, you consent to receive recurring marketing and informational communications from Affinity Cellular by phone call and text message — including communications sent using an automatic telephone dialing system or prerecorded or artificial voice — at the number you provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out at any time by replying STOP (or END, CANCEL, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE) to any text message. After you opt out, we will send one final message confirming your opt-out.

For help, reply HELP to any text message, email help@affinitycellular.com, or call (833) 679-1090.

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

You may also opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link included in every marketing email we send.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, measure the performance of our advertising, and understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect how the site functions.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites, including the main Affinity Cellular website and carrier policy pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Depending on your state of residence (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, Utah, and Virginia), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses of your information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
