Return Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Affinity Cellular® wants you to be completely comfortable with your purchase. Every phone purchase is covered by our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee, described below.

1. The 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

You may return your phone for your money back within 30 days of purchase, provided your total usage stays under all three of these limits:

• 30 minutes of talk time (incoming and outgoing combined)
• 30 text messages (sent and received combined)
• 30 MB of data

The guarantee ends as soon as you pass any one of those limits, or 30 days after purchase — whichever comes first.

2. What is refunded

If your return meets the conditions above, your refund includes:

  • The activation fee;
  • The cost of your handset; and
  • The prorated cost of your service plan.

3. What is not refunded

The cost of any additional services purchased on your account is not refundable.

4. How to start a return

Call customer support at (833) 679-1090 or email help@affinitycellular.com within your 30-day window. Our team will confirm your eligibility and walk you through returning your phone for your refund.

Support hours (CT): Monday–Friday 8am–10pm · Saturday 8am–8pm · Sunday 10am–7pm.
